moon_temp_tracer — Wheatstone-Bridge Sensor Circuit (Rev D)

Replaces the single-ended divider in moon_temp_ads1115 with a bridge + LM4040 excitation feeding the ADS1115 differential inputs. Two identical sensor units share one I²C bus via ADDR-pin address selection. Sensor boards are power-gated from the host (⑥) — only energized during the read window.

AIN2 is unused. ADS1115 only supports four fixed differential pairs — AIN0-AIN1, AIN0-AIN3, AIN1-AIN3, AIN2-AIN3 — so putting the rail monitor on AIN0 (single-ended) forces the bridge pair onto AIN1-AIN3.

Critical notes

  • R1, R2, R3 and the LM4040 all sit on the sensor board, exposed to the same -30°C..45°C swing as the NTC. RNCF0603TKY10K0 (0.01%, ±5ppm/°C) comfortably exceeds the 0.1%/≤25ppm/°C this design called for — use the tight-tempco (A-grade, ≤15ppm/°C) LM4040 variant to match.
  • AIN0 is wired straight to the Vexc bus — the same net that feeds the top of R1 and R3 — so it reads the excitation/rail voltage directly, not a divided-down value.
  • PGA ±2.048V on the bridge channel gives ~2.2× headroom over the -30°C extreme (max swing ≈0.93V) — avoids clipping from thermistor tolerance/self-heating. The AIN0 rail read uses ±4.096V for headroom above the 2.048V nominal.
  • Thermal epoxy is electrically non-conductive; the PCB is additionally isolated from the steel bar with kapton tape layers.
  • Temperature must be recovered by inverting the actual bridge equation for R_NTC (not a linear approximation), then the Beta equation with B=3988K (B57861S0103F045) — the bridge is non-linear across this range just like the NTC itself.
  • Sensor boards are power-gated (⑥) from SWITCHED_3V3, not always-on — firmware must wait ≥5τ (~50-100ms) after enabling the branch before the first ADS1115 read, for the LM4040/bridge to settle.
